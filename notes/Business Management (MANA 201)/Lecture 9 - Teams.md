# Groups

>NOTE: Group and team will be used interchangeably

*→ Group Dynamics: characteristics and processes that determine how groups and teams' function and how effective they will be 
Five key elements:*

1. Group size and roles 
2. Group leadership 
3. Stages of group development 
4. Group norms 
5. Group cohesiveness

## Group Size

#### Smaller groups (2-9 members):

- Interact more with each other and to coordinate efforts 
- Be more motivated, satisfied and committed 
- Find it easier to share information 
- Be better able to see the importance of their personal contributions

#### Larger groups (>10 members):

- More resources to achieve group goals (knowledge, time, etc.) 
- Division of labour: splitting the work into particular tasks and assigning tasks to individuals

## Group Roles

**Two major types of roles:** 

1. Task-oriented roles: performed by group members to make sure the task gets done 
2. Maintenance roles: ensure that team members have good relationships

## Group Leadership

- Managers may assume the leadership role 
- May appoint a member of a group who is not a manager (formal leader) 
- Group or team members may choose their own leaders 
- Leader may emerge naturally (informal)

## Group Norms

**Group Norms → shared guidelines and rules for behaviour that members follow.** 

*Group members conform to norms for these reasons:* 
1. To obtain rewards / avoid punishments 
2. To imitate group members they like/ respect 
3. They believe it is the right way to behave 

*Failure to conform, groups response:*
1. Try to get the member to change their ways 
2. Expel the member. 
3. Change norm to be consistent with behaviour.

## Group Decision Making

*→ Groupthink: pattern of faulty decision making that occurs when members strive for agreement at the expense of accurately assessing relevant information*

**Three effective group decision-making techniques:** 
1. *Brainstorming →* problem-solving in which individuals meet face-to-face to generate and debate a wide variety of alternatives 
2. *Nominal Group Technique →* group members write down solutions, read suggestions, discuss and then rank the alternatives 
3. *Delphi Technique →* group members do not meet face-to-face but respond in writing to questions posed by group leader

## Group Conflict

*social loafing →* tendency of individuals to put forth less effort when they work in groups than when working alone

*Task-related →* members of group perceive a problem or have disagreement about nature of task / project 

*Relationship →* members of group perceive each other’s attitudes as a problem 

*Organizational →* discord that arises when goals, interests, or values of different individuals or groups are incompatible

## Conflict Resolution Approaches

**Five conflict-handling behaviours:** 
1. *Avoiding –* withdrawing from conflict 
2. *Competing –* satisfying one’s own interests, without regard to interests of the other party 
3. *Compromising –* parties willing to engage in a give and-take exchange and to make concessions until resolution reached 
4. *Accommodating –* pleasing the other party by putting the other’s interests ahead of his/her own 
5. *Collaborating –* resolve differences in a way that makes both parties better off
