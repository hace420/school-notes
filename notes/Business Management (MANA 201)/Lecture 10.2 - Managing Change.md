# Organizational Change and Culture

**Two ways for organizational change to come about:**

1. Induced Change: Planned new institutional arrangements and policies made in response to changes in the task-specific or general environment to gain a competitive advantage 
- *Example: mergers and acquisition*

2. Imposed Change: New institutional arrangements made in response to changes in legislation, law or shareholder activism                      	
- *Example: response to legal challenges*

# What can Managers to Reduce Resistance to Change?

- Clearly communicate future state and seek input 
- Increase the driving forces 
- Remove some of the restraining forces 
- When resistant extremely high, use both driving and restraining forces for unfreezing 
- Use formal power to withhold rewards (last resort
