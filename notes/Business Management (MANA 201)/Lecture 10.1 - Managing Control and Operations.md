# What is Organizational Control?

## 1. Establish Standards for Performance 
- Standards are criteria against which results are measured
- Includes: Efficiency, Quality, Responsiveness to customers, Innovation 
- Types: Output standards, operating costs, behavioural standards

## 2. Measure Performance
- the actual outputs that result from the behaviour of their members (output control) 
- the behaviours themselves (behavioural control)

## 3. Compare Actual Performance with Expected Performance 
- If performance is higher than expected, are performance standards are too low? 
- If performance is lower than expected, managers must decide whether the deviation requires corrective action

## 4. Correct Deviations 
- Focus on the root of the problem 
- Causes: 

	➢ The standard was too high 
	➢ They need to change the way in which resources (e.g. technology) are being used 
	➢ Lack of training 
	➢ Organization needs restructuring

# Control Systems in Production Process

- Control Systems: target-setting, monitoring, evaluation, feedback to provide information about whether the organization’s strategy and structure are working effectively and efficiently

# Problems with Output Control

1. Extreme difficult goals may not motivate
2. Inappropriate goals can lead to short-term emphasis
3. Unachievable goals can lead to unethical behavior
4. may not be responsive enough if conditions change

# Types of Behavioural Controls 

*Behavioural Controls: →* Mechanisms to keep employee behaviour on track and make org. structures work as they are designed
