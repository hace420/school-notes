## Skills

**Three types of principal skills acquired through education, training, experience** 
1. **Conceptual Skills:** ability to analyze and diagnose a situation and to distinguish between cause and effect. 
2. **Interpersonal Skills:** ability to understand, alter, lead, control the behaviour of other individuals/ groups. 
3. **Technical Skills:** job-specific knowledge and techniques that are required to perform an organizational role
