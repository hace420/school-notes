# Values,Attitude,Moods & Emotions

> → **Values:** describe what managers are trying to achieve through work and how they think they should behave 
> 
> → **Attitudes:** capture thoughts and feelings about their specific jobs and organizations 
> 
> → **Moods and Emotions**: how managers actually feel when they are managing

## Values: Terminal & Instrumental

1. *Terminal Value:* personal conviction about lifelong goals or objectives

2. *Instrumental Value:* personal conviction about desired modes of conduct or ways of behaving
---
## Attitudes

**→ Two most important in this context are:** 

1. *Job Satisfaction:* feelings and beliefs managers have about their current job 
		→ More likely to go the extra mile
		→ Less likely to quit
		
2. *Organizational Commitment:* feelings and beliefs that managers have about their organization as a whole
---

## Moods and Emotions

*→ **Mood:** feeling or state of mind*

*→ **Emotions:** more intense, often directly linked to whatever caused the emotion and more short-lived*

*→ **Emotional Intelligence:** ability to understand and manage one’s own moods and emotions and the moods and emotions of other people*

## Locus of Control

→ *How much control you have over what happens to and around you*

- **Internal locus of control:** Believe you are responsible for your own fate; see your own actions and behaviours as being major determinants of important outcomes 

- **External locus of control:** Believe that outside forces are responsible for what happens to and around you; you do not think your own actions make much of a difference
