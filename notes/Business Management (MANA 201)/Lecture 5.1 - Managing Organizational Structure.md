# Grouping tasks into jobs

## Job design 

- Job Design → Process: managers decide how  to divide the work to be done into specific jobs
- Work Specialization → degree to which the job is focused on particular tasks or multiple tasks

## Work specialization

- Job Simplification → Reduces number of tasks each worker performs
- Job Enlargement → Increases number of different tasks for a given
- job by changing the division of labour
- Job Enrichment → Increases degree of responsibility

# Departmentalization

May be divided by:
	- Functional Structure - composed of all the departments that an organization requires to produce its goods and services
	- Divisional Structures - composed of separate business units, functions that work together. Goal is to create smaller, more manageable units
		- Product Divisions 
		- Geographic Division
		- Market Segment Division

## Simple Structure

- Simple Structure → organizational structure where the owner is the general manager responsible for the activities in all the functions
![[Pasted image 20261011142148.png]]

## Team Based Org. Structure: Matrix Teams

- Matrix Team → organizational structure that simultaneously groups people and resources by function (e.g. marketing, R&D, production, etc.) and by product
- Results in a complex network of reporting relationships
- Very flexible and can respond rapidly to change
- Each employee reports to two managers - for example:
	- Functional manager
	- Manager of product team

**Product teams:** empowered and accountable for making most of  the important decisions involved in product development.

**Product team manager**: acts as a facilitator, controlling the financial resources and keeping the project on time and within budget.

**Functional managers:** ensure that the product is the best that it can be in order to make the most of its differentiated appeal.

![[Pasted image 20261011142447.png]]

## Team Based Org. Structure: Product / Project Teams

**Product / Project Team:** employees are permanently assigned to cross-functional
team and report only to the project team manager

- Group of individuals brought together from different departments to perform organizational tasks
- Removes dual-reporting relationships for employees
![[Pasted image 20261011142725.png]]

## B2B Network Structures 

- Network Structure: Series of global strategic alliances that an organization creates with suppliers, manufacturers and/or distributors to produce and market a product
- Strategic Alliance: Commits two or more companies to exchange or share resources in order to produce and market a product

# Choosing a Formal or Flexible Overall Structure

![[Pasted image 20261011142939.png]]

- In general, the higher the level of uncertainty associated with these factors, the more appropriate a flexible, adaptable structure is as opposed to a formal, rigid one
- According to **contingency theory** managers design organizational structures to fit the factors or circumstances that are affecting the company the most and causing them the most uncertainty

## Factors Affecting Choice of Overall Organizational Structure

### 1. Organizational Environment

- **Quickly changing external environment**  (greater uncertainty), leads to greater problems a manager faces in gaining access to scare resources
	- requires flexibility
- **Stable external environment** (lower uncertainty), leads to less coordination and communication needed to obtain resources
	- require formality

### 2. Strategy

- **Differentiation Strategy** - usually succeeds best with a **flexible structure**
- **Low-Cost Strategy** - does best in more **formal structure**

### 3. Technology

**Technology**: Combination of skills, knowledge, tools, machines, computers, and equipment that
are used to design, produce, and distribute goods and services.

- **More complicated technology requires more flexible structure**
- **More routine technology requires a more formal structure**

### 4. Human Resources

More likely to be flexible or decentralized when: 
	- People are required to work together in groups or teams
	- Employees are highly skilled
