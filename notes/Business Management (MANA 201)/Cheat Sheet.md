a vision statement reveals the big picture of the organization and its dream for the future.

a mission statement acommplishes 
	1. state the broad purpose of the company
	2. differentiate the company from competitors
	3. identify a compnays products and customers

5 steps of planning 
	1. determine vision and gaols
	2. conducts environmental analysis 
	3. formulate strategy
	4. implement stratehy
	5. evaluate strategy.

plans should have 
	1. flexibility
	2. accuracy
	3. unity
	4. continuity

the type of plan that defines an organization mission and goals overall strategy and structure is the corporate-level plan. Also indicated the markets in which the company will compete in and why.

In a business-level plan managers of each division detail out long term divisional goals that are in line with corporate goals and various strategies and structures necessary to achieve divisional goals.
	4 strategies:
		1. cost leadership
		2. focus low cost
		3. focused differentiation
		4. differentiation
		

functional-level plans state the goals managers of each functions will use to achieve business and corporate goals

generally speaking corporate and business level goals require long and intermediate term plans while functional level goals require intermediate and short term
