# Paired Data Sets

- We are often concerned with data sets that consist of pairs of values that have some relationship to each other. If each element in such a data set has an x value and a y value, then we represent the i-th data point by the pair (xi , yi ).


## Sample Correlation Coefficient

Let Sx and Sy denote, respectively, the sample standard deviations of the x values and the y values. The sample correlation coefficient, r, of the data pairs (xi , yi ), i =1, …, n is defined by

![[Pasted image 20261112112030.png]]

> When r > 0 we say that the sample data pairs are positively correlated, and when r < 0 we say that they are negatively correlated.


- The absolute value of the sample correlation coefficient r (that is, |r|, its value without regard to its sign) is a measure of the strength of the linear relationship between the x and the y values of a data pair. 
- The sign of r gives the direction of the relation.
- Correlation measures association, not causation since there may be other factors that effect the correlation.
