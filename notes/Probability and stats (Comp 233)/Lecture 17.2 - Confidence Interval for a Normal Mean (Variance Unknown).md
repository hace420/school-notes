# Z vs t Summary

*→ Known σ (underlying distribution is normal or n > 30): 
- Use Z 
- Narrower intervals 
- Less uncertainty

*→ Unknown σ (n < 40): 
- Use t 
- Wider intervals 
- More uncertainty
