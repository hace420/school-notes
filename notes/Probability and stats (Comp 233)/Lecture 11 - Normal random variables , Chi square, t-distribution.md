## Approximating other distributions 

#### Binomial distribution

- A binomial distribution with n and p is approximately normal for large n and p not too close to 1 or 0. The approximating normal distribution has 
```
μ = np, σ^2 = np(1 − p).
```

#### Poisson distribution

- Poisson distribution with parameter λ is approximately normal for large λ. The approximating normal distribution has parameters
```
μ = σ^2 = λ
```
